Common questions

How do I talk to the assistant?

On Mac, hold the Space bar while the app is open, speak, and release. On iPhone and Apple Watch, press and hold the talk button. You can also type.

How do screenshot questions work?

On Mac, press ⌘N to capture your screen and get an instant answer about what's on it (you can change the shortcut in Settings). The first use asks for macOS Screen Recording permission.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Manage or cancel in your device's subscription settings: System Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on Mac, or Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone. Purchases can be restored from the paywall's "Restore purchase" button.

How do I delete my account?

Use Delete Account in the app's Settings, or email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you. See the Privacy Policy for what deletion covers.
